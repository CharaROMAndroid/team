Who I Am

I’m Garry Strait, a nonbinary developer (I also go by Chara) who thrives somewhere between structured engineering and controlled chaos.

I build things because I can, refine them because I care, and occasionally break them just to understand them better. I’m deeply hands-on, very systems-oriented, and I tend to learn by diving headfirst into problems most people would rather avoid.

I’m not interested in just using technology; I want to understand it, bend it, and sometimes rebuild it entirely in my own image.

You can find me on XDA as BunnyPad-Dev, or in my Telegram chat: https://t.me/bunnypaddev

Core Traits

  • Practical over theoretical - If it works and I understand why, it’s good.
  • Detail-obsessed when it matters - Especially with systems, builds, and low-level behavior.
  • Comfortable in chaos - Whether it’s debugging a broken build environment or designing something unconventional.

Notable Projects

CharaROM

A custom Android ROM that blends technical stability with a very intentional design philosophy: “Uniquely Chaotic.”

CharaROM isn’t just another AOSP derivative, it’s:

  • A fully functional Android distribution
  • A platform for experimentation
  • A reflection of personal identity and creative direction

Highlights:

  • Custom features beyond standard ROM expectations
  • Unique branding and identity (not just a reskin)
  • Experimental UI elements, including unconventional font integrations
  • Clean build pipeline and OTA packaging system

It started as a way to avoid dealing with certain people.
It became something I’m genuinely proud of.

BunnyPad

A Python-based notepad application focused on simplicity and reliability.

Instead of trying to be a full IDE, BunnyPad is designed around:

  • Ease of use for non-technical users
  • Stability over feature bloat
  • Clean, predictable behavior

Ironically, despite its simplicity, it ended up being recognized in places I never expected, like Android modding communities.

Kernel / Android Development Work

  • Building and modifying Android kernels (including NetHunter-related work)
  • Debugging low-level issues (boot loops, kernel panics, linker failures)
  • Working with Soong, Ninja, and the Android build system
  • Handling real-world device quirks (hardware faults, vendor inconsistencies)

I don’t just build Android, I fight it sometimes.

Technical Focus Areas
