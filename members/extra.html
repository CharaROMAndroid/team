External Contributors

Psi

The source of motivation to keep striving to do better. Every build has a little bit of her in it. (Please do note that Psi does have melanoma and Chara is doing her best to get Psi the treatment she needs)

crDroid

Our upstream. CharaROM is built on crDroid, so a huge amount of what you use every day is their work.

chrisaw/RestlessOS

Helped with some of the privacy, security, and stability patches. All patches from here are labeled as RESTLESSOS PATCH due to the fact they are distributed as .patch files. RestlessOS is an unofficial, unaffiliated fork of GrapheneOS, and Chara does do minor contributions to RestlessOS.

GrapheneOS

Source for some of the privacy and security enhancements, the base for the CharaROM Messages app (in development), the PdfViewer application, and a reference for how to implement certain functions. We thank them for their hard work and the inspiration as a result of their work. [Due to unclear logo usage terms, Chara had Kat make an alternative logo to avoid any conflict with GrapheneOS.]

AviumUI

Most of our recovery's UI was made by them. We thank them for their work!

BlissROM

Helped with adding the codename section to CharaROM.

YAAP

Our camera app, Aperture, came from their tree, including MediaTek HFPS 60 FPS recording and the OPLUS HDR fix. We've since moved back to LineageOS Aperture, but those two fixes are coming with us.

CalyxOS

A big chunk of our privacy and security features came from here: netd, Global VPN, USB restrictions, camera and microphone timeouts, timeout reboot, Datura, and the EmergencyButton panic button.

CaesiumOS

The Settings easter egg and our Updater's OTA configuration. Also the ROM of our very own JPB, and part of why CharaROM exists at all.

DivestOS

Vendor defaults, the LowRAM/Go targets, and removing analytics from LineageParts.

Evolution X

Boot animation styles, the Compose selector rewrite, and the fix for enabling cloned apps.

Project CiRCLE

Conditional charging sounds, the SBC-HD toggle strings, and the Chaos Engineer preference.

EunoiaOS

The vendor change that disables StorageManager.

LMODroid

Helped with including Ripple in our builds.
